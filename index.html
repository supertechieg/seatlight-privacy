Seatlight privacy

Last updated October 23, 2026

In short: your plans and guest lists stay on your devices and in your own iCloud. We never see them. Seatlight has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send your data to any server of ours.

What Seatlight keeps, and where

  • Your plans: the venue, tables, seats, guest names and the notes you add. They're stored inside the app on your device.
  • iCloud: if you're signed in to iCloud, your plans also sync through your own iCloud account (Apple's private database for the app), so your other devices get them. Only you can read that copy.
  • Backups: a daily copy of each plan, and a copy before big changes, kept for 14 days in your iCloud Drive (Files › iCloud Drive › Seatlight), or on your device if iCloud Drive is off.
  • Sharing: if you share a plan, the people you invite can see and change that plan. You can stop sharing at any time.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to scan a room's walls, doors and windows. The scan is measured on your device.
  • Contacts: when you add guests from Contacts, only the people you pick are added. Seatlight doesn't read the rest of your contacts.
  • Photos and files: only the picture or file you choose, such as a floor plan.
  • Maps: when you look up your venue's address on the satellite map, the search goes to Apple Maps.

Testing with TestFlight

While Seatlight is in testing, Apple's TestFlight may send us crash reports and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. These come through Apple and never include your plans unless you put them in a screenshot.

Deleting your data

Delete a plan in the app. Deleted plans are kept on your device so you can put them back; deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device. Backups in iCloud Drive can be deleted in the Files app. To remove the iCloud copy of your plans, go to Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Account Storage › Seatlight.

Children

Seatlight isn't aimed at children and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Seatlight is made by an independent developer. Questions: open an issue at github.com/supertechieg/seatlight-privacy, or use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight.
